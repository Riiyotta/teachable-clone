Frequently asked questions about Teachable

What is Teachable?

Teachable is an all-in-one platform for experts and businesses to create and sell online courses, coaching, memberships, and digital downloads. It includes a course builder with AI tools, iOS and Android student apps, and Teachable Payments, a built-in checkout that handles sales tax, VAT, and GST. Teachable is part of Hotmart Company.

How does Teachable work?

You create your products with Teachable's course builder, customize your school with your own brand and domain, and set your pricing. Students buy through Teachable Payments and access your content on the web or in the iOS and Android apps, while Teachable handles hosting, payments, and taxes behind the scenes. You can get started with a 7-day free trial.

Who uses Teachable?

More than 150,000 creators, experts, and businesses use Teachable, from language teachers and finance professionals to fitness coaches and companies training their teams. What they have in common is a commitment to education: Teachable helps them turn real-world expertise into thriving education businesses.

What can I sell on Teachable?

You can sell online courses, one-on-one or group coaching, digital downloads like templates and guides, and memberships with recurring subscriptions. You can also bundle products, add a community for your students, and offer upsells and order bumps at checkout.

Is Teachable legit?

Yes. Teachable is part of Hotmart Company and is used by more than 150,000 experts and businesses. Together, Teachable and Hotmart creators have earned more than $10 billion, and students in 180 countries learn on Teachable.

Is Teachable free?

No, Teachable doesn't have a free plan. Every self-serve plan (Starter, Builder, and Growth) includes a 7-day free trial and a 30-day money-back guarantee, so you can test the platform before committing. See current prices on our pricing page.

Are there any transaction fees?

Only on the Starter plan, which has a 7.5% transaction fee. Builder, Growth, and Custom plans have 0% transaction fees when you use Teachable Payments. Standard payment processing fees may still apply.

Can I change my plan once I sign up?

Yes. You can change your plan at any time from your school's admin. You'll be charged the new plan amount at your next billing date.

Can I cancel my account at any time?

Yes, you can cancel your Teachable account at any time. If you're within your first 30 days, you can also request a refund under our 30-day money-back guarantee.
