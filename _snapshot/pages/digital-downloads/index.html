Frequently asked questions

How much does Teachable cost per month?

Your first seven days are free. After that, we have plans starting at $29 per month, each designed to fit your unique business needs. Browse plans and pricing here.

Why should I sell digital downloads on Teachable?

Because it’s one of the fastest, easiest ways to turn your content into revenue. When you create a digital download on Teachable, you can sell everything from templates and guides to audio files and spreadsheets—no extra tools or tech required. Just upload, price, publish, done.

How do I create a digital download on Teachable?

Once you’re logged in, navigate to the “Digital Downloads” tab in the admin dashboard and hit “Create digital download.” Then, add information about your product (like a title and description), upload your file (or link to something like a Google Doc or Canva design), and hit publish. That’s it—you’re ready to set your price and start selling.

What are digital downloads, exactly?

Digital downloads are downloadable files (including PDFs, spreadsheets, audio files, templates, and more) that your audience can use right away. They’re quick to deliver, cost nothing to ship, and can be sold again and again.

What’s the best type of digital download to sell?

Anything that saves time, solves a problem, or adds value. Templates, swipe files, toolkits, budget planners, and guides are all strong performers among the many types of digital downloads. If it’s helpful to your audience, chances are they’ll want to buy it.

Can I really make money selling digital downloads?

Definitely. They’re one of the easiest ways to earn passive income. You create it once, and keep selling it. Plus, they work great on their own (as upsells, order bumps, or free lead magnets) or bundled alongside your other products—like courses, coaching, or memberships.

How many digital downloads can I sell?

Our plans scale with your usage needs, so we grow together as your business becomes more successful. Check out our plans to see which fits your needs.

What else can I sell on Teachable?

In addition to digital downloads, you can sell online courses, coaching, memberships, community spaces, and more—all in one place.
