Frequently asked questions

Can I cancel my account at any time?

Yes, you can cancel your Teachable account at any time. If you're within your first 30 days, you can also request a refund under our 30-day money-back guarantee.

Can I change my plan once I sign up?

Yes. You can change your plan at any time from your school's admin. You'll be charged the new plan amount at your next billing date.

Are there any transaction fees?

Only on the Starter plan, which has a 7.5% transaction fee. Builder, Growth, and Custom plans have 0% transaction fees when you use Teachable Payments. Standard payment processing fees may still apply.

Who uses Teachable?

More than 150,000 creators, experts, and businesses use Teachable, from language teachers and finance professionals to fitness coaches and companies training their teams. What they have in common is a commitment to education: Teachable helps them turn real-world expertise into thriving education businesses.

What counts as a product?

A published product is any course, coaching offering, or digital download that you make live on your Teachable school. Each one counts as 1 product toward your plan limit — regardless of how many videos, modules, or files it contains. A course with 30 videos and 5 quizzes = 1 published product. Memberships and bundles don't count toward your product limit. Unpublished drafts don't count either.

Is there a free plan?

Teachable is a paid service that includes a 7-day free trial and a 30-day money-back guarantee. That means you can explore Teachable at no cost before committing.

Are prices listed in USD?

Yes, all prices on this page are in US dollars (USD), but Teachable supports global payments — your students can pay in their local currency.

Can I pay monthly instead of annually?

Yes! Use the Monthly / Yearly toggle at the top of this page to switch to monthly billing. Annual billing saves you 22% compared to paying month-to-month.
